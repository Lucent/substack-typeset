\part{Coherence under recomposition}
\epigraph{When the usual narratives stop holding, you start trusting what survives translation: patterns that remain recoverable after you change coordinates, swap formalisms, or retell the story from another angle. Identity and meaning flatten into equivalence classes---different descriptions pointing at the same underlying object---so the ``real'' is whatever stays stable under recomposition. Coherence becomes not a virtue signal but a selection rule: what you can keep using after the rewrite.}{}
