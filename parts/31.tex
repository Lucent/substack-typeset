\part{Taste that scales}
\epigraph{Taste is what your perceptual system converges to after enough exposure, once size, novelty, and status stop masquerading as quality. Scale sharpens detection of fake coherence and rewards what survives many eyes and many incentives, which is why ``unspoiled'' and ``time-tested'' start to feel trustworthy. Where taste converges, hits get mined: the space has attractors, and broad contact just finds them faster.}{}
