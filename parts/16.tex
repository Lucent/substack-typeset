\part{Text's power and its ceiling}
\epigraph{Text feels like power because it can steer attention and coordinate action, especially when a system seems to read you at high resolution. But its strongest effects often come from selection and routing rather than persuasion, because language is compression: it gestures at a shared world-model while dropping detail. So the limit is built in---words can shape the path through the map, but they can't replace the map.}{}
