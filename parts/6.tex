\part{Politics and belief}
\epigraph{In high-conflict settings, belief becomes a defensive posture: you pick frames that keep you righteous, then facts get filtered by what they would obligate you to admit. As a result, ``is'' and ``ought'' tangle---claims arrive bundled with identity and moral verdicts---so disagreement becomes hard to unwind without someone losing innocence. The stable move is to look for hinge structure: the few factual or causal pivots that would actually change the conclusion, and the hidden-variable moves that make bundles look inevitable.}{}
