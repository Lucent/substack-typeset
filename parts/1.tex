\part{Persona under exposure}
\epigraph{Personality is a set of constraints that turns you into something legible to other people, and those constraints either survive scale or get rewritten by the incentives of being watched. As visibility grows, conflict stops being local and symmetric: actions become public objects with durable downstream effects, so ``repair'' matters less than choosing rules whose failure modes are recoverable. The goal is to pick a status-invariant program---principles you'd still endorse after role swaps, audience flips, and power tilts---so your self stays coherent when the arena changes.}{}
