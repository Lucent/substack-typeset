\part{Feelings as control layer}
\epigraph{Feelings are control signals emitted by your world-model, not decorations on top of it, so treating them as mere noise loses the steering layer. If you optimize the feeling directly you Goodhart the instrument, and then the feedback becomes useless---especially in a branching life where the job is to pick workable local moves without pretending you can command the whole tree. The practical skill is to follow interrupts, learn from surprise upsides, and decompose big wants into smaller drives you can actually steer.}{}
